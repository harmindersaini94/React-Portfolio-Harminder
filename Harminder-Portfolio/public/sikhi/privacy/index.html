Privacy policy — Sikhi

Last updated 7 November 2026.

Who we are

Sikhi is a scripture and learning app. It does not create an account for you. Contact: hssaini2709@gmail.com.

Data that stays on your device

Bookmarks, notes, Nitnem progress, reading position, search history, simran counts, and settings are stored only on the device (and, on iOS, in the app's local storage). Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data, deletes them. We do not operate a server that holds this information, so there is nothing of yours for us to delete remotely.

Location

Approximate location is used only if you open Nearby Gurdwaras. The app asks for approximate location (not precise, and not in the background). That location is sent to the OpenStreetMap Overpass service to look up nearby Sikh places of worship. We do not store it, and we do not send it anywhere else.

Scripture and audio requests

When you read Gurbani, Nitnem, or the Hukamnama, the app requests text from the BaniDB API. Nitnem audio is streamed from Wikimedia Commons and archive.org. Those services see the usual connection data, including your IP address, as part of answering the request. We do not add an account identifier to those requests.

What we do not do

The app has no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK, and no tracking. It does not use the advertising identifier. It does not sell data. It does not ask you to sign in.

Children

The app is a reference and education app. It is not directed at children under 13 as a kids product, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will change. The copy shipped inside the app repository is the one to host.
